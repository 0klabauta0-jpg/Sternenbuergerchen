## Originalprompt

Create one polished game UI art-direction concept board for the existing German sci-fi strategy game STERNENBÜRGERCHEN. This is a DESIGN PROPOSAL, not a runtime screenshot. Use the references for its warm low-poly moon colony and dark blue petrol HUD context, but redesign the ugly plain forms into elegant premium readable management windows. Wide high-resolution board, 3 columns by 2 rows, six distinct legible desktop window designs labeled exactly: 'Smart Mobile', 'Bauen', 'Werkstatt · Blueprints', 'Lager & Ausrüstung', 'Datenterminal', 'Schiff'. Consistent design language across all six: deep desaturated navy-teal background, inset slate cards, creamy readable type, restrained turquoise 1px borders and small amber section codes, generous structured spacing. Not neon, no holographic clutter, no glowing illegible text, no fake 3D desktop devices. Flat sharp game HUD. Each window has a compact uppercase eyebrow, bold German title, one explanatory subtitle, small right-hand status badge, main content area with structured rows/cards, clearly separated bottom action area. Smart Mobile has mission cards and a selected satellite recovery description; Bauen has 2nd landing pad card with small low-poly pad illustration and 100 Cr cost; Werkstatt has three blueprint cards with armor, mining tool, rifle and visible locked states; Lager & Ausrüstung shows an entire little low-poly astronaut with armor slot at torso and primary pistol and secondary medic tool at hips, beside remaining stock grid; Datenterminal shows a simple tasteful satellite-chip illustration, a clear empty state and 'Missionsdaten hochladen' button; Schiff shows a Cutlass-shaped small chunky twin-engine transport illustration and crisp fuel/crew/service rows. Do not invent gameplay functions beyond these, no character skill editors, no currencies besides credits. Coherent visually attractive visual hierarchy, practical reusable panels that can be implemented as real Godot controls. Keep the board uncluttered and let the six layouts carry the image.
