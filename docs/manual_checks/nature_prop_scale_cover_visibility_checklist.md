# Nature Prop Scale Cover Visibility Checklist

- Open `maps/editor_terrain_visual_recovery_reference.json`.
- Confirm Nature Tree Blocker reads as tall LOS-blocking cover.
- Confirm Nature Rock Cover is large enough to read as hard cover.
- Confirm Nature Bush Cover is visible but still reads as soft, walkable cover.
- Confirm Nature Cactus Cover is visible on sand and does not look like a full wall.
- Confirm Nature Deco Logs remain small visual detail and do not imply hard cover.
- Confirm every prop sits on the current tile top at H0/H1/H2.
- Confirm grid and selection remain usable around props without drawing through them.
