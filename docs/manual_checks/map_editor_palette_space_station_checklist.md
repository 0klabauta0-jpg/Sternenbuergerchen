# Map Editor Palette Space Station Checklist

- Open the map editor and inspect the left palette groups.
- Confirm Terrain, Nature Boden, Nature Props, Waende, Cliffs, Rampen, Felsen and Space Station appear in order.
- Click the Space Station group and confirm only station assets appear.
- Place a station computer and station container on the reference map.
- Confirm the resources load as local `res://Assets/curated/kenney/space_station/...` assets.
- Confirm Space Station objects show their shared colormap/atlas texture instead of plain white or grey material.
- Confirm the station objects do not change terrain height or surface unless explicitly painted separately.
- Confirm no absolute local source path appears in the data catalog.
